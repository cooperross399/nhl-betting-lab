**The rule.** A skater with 60+ points, 25+ goals or 55+ assists last regular season is listed in that category when his drought reaches either the tier bar from last season's total (points 100+ -> 2, 85-99 -> 3, 70-84 -> 4, 60-69 -> 5; goals 40+ -> 4, 25-39 -> 8; assists 55+ -> 3) or, for points and assists, the surprise bar from his own prior-season hit rate p, the smallest n with (1 - p)^n <= 0.05; the wager is the over 0.5.

**The bars.** Tier bar, from last season's total: points 60+ -> 2; goals 40+ -> 4, 25-39 -> 8; assists 60+ -> 1. Surprise bar, from his own prior-season hit rate p (games with one or more / games dressed): the smallest n with (1 - p)^n <= 0.05 (undefined when p is 0; 1 when p is 1). A player is listed when his drought reaches either. The flat rule shipped that morning (#307: every qualifier at 5+) is kept below as `RULE: drought 5+` so the history reads the same.

**How it is measured.** 2024-25 and 2025-26 regular seasons. One wager per selection at the best price any book offered. `card` is each event's first snapshot (the window the Gameday card prices in); `late` is its last. Interval: 95%, bootstrapped over game nights. MDE: the smallest ROI this sample could detect at 80% power. Seasons are labelled by their starting year.
